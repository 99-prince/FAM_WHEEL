About

Connecting farms, moving crops, growing futures

FAM WHEEL was built to close the gap between the farmer's field and the buyer's warehouse. Too much value is lost to unnecessary middlemen and unreliable transport — so we built a marketplace where farmers list once, buyers discover directly, and verified transport partners move the harvest, all tracked in one place.
